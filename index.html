Questions

Things people usually ask us

Do you repair the sofa at my home, or take it away?
Almost every repair happens at your home: frames, springs, foam, recliners, leather patches. For a full fabric change we sometimes take the cushions or a section to our workshop for a day. Pickup and drop are free within the areas listed above.
How much does sofa repair cost in Noida?
Most single repairs land between ₹700 and ₹2,500. Cushion refilling starts at ₹499 a seat. A full fabric change on a 5-seater is usually ₹11,000 – ₹16,000 including mid-range fabric. Send photos on WhatsApp and we'll give you a real number for your sofa.
Do I have to pay anything in advance?
Not for repairs. You pay after the job, once you've checked it. For a fabric change we ask you to pay only for the fabric once you've picked it, because it gets cut to your sofa's size. Labour is always paid at the end.
What does the 6-month warranty cover?
Our workmanship: stitching, joints we fixed, springs and webbing we replaced, and recliner mechanisms we repaired. If any of it gives way within 6 months, we come back and fix it free. It doesn't cover new damage like pet scratches, cuts, burns or stains.
How long does the work take?
Small repairs take 1–3 hours. A recliner usually takes 1–2 hours. Foam refilling for a 5-seater takes about half a day. A full fabric change takes 1–2 days depending on the sofa.
Can you fix a sofa bought from a big brand or online store?
Yes. We regularly work on sofas from showrooms, online furniture stores and local markets. Recliner mechanisms are fairly standard, and if a part has to be replaced we tell you the part cost before ordering it.
How do I know the foam and fabric are good quality?
Before we start, we tell you the foam density and the fabric rate per metre, and send them to you on WhatsApp. You can feel the foam sample and see the fabric in person.
Do you also make new sofas?
Yes. If your old sofa isn't worth repairing, we can make a new one to your measurements in the fabric you like. Ask us on WhatsApp for designs and a quote.
